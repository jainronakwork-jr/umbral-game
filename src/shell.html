How to Play

←→ or AD

Walk. Walk into crates to push them.

Space or W

Jump. Hold for a higher jump.

E or ↓

Pull levers.

Esc

Pause.

Gamepad

Stick to walk, A to jump, X to pull. On phones, use the on-screen buttons.

The rules

Thorns, traps, water, blades and falling stones end you at once. You return to the last place you stood safely. Crushers and saws move in rhythm, so watch before you run. When the Dark gives chase, do not stop.
